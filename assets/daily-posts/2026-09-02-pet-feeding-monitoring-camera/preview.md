## Pet Feeding & Monitoring Camera — Voice-Alert Edition

**Board:** Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W — Quad-core Linux SBC (WiFi + BT, camera connector, small form factor)
**Date:** 2026-09-02

### Overview
A camera module streams live video to a small web dashboard while a servo-driven hopper dispenses a measured portion of food on a schedule or on remote trigger. Because the Zero 2 W has enough headroom to run a lightweight web server and encode a video stream simultaneously, the whole feeder plus monitor fits on one board. It's a practical build for checking on a pet during a long day at work. Status changes trigger a spoken alert through a small speaker, which is a lot harder to miss than a dashboard notification.

### Key Components / Peripherals
- Raspberry Pi Camera Module
- Servo-driven food hopper
- Small speaker / audio amp module
